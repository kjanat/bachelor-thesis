This application aligns with the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam's academic integrity guidelines. It served as a supplementary resource in ensuring the thesis met high standards of academic writing. Additionally, the AI language model was a valuable asset in organizing and structuring the content, which helped in presenting a coherent and logically sequenced argument. This approach was adopted not to replace personal input or traditional research methods, but to augment the overall quality and readability of the thesis.
