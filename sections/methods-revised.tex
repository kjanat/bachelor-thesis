\section{Methods}\label{s:methods}

This systematic review was conducted in accordance with the \newterm{Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis} (PRISMA) 2020 guidelines \cite{page2021}. A comprehensive literature search targeted articles published in English across two primary databases: \stress{PubMed} and \stress{ScienceDirect}. The search was conducted for articles published up to 1st January 2024, with PubMed serving as the principal database.

\subsection{Search strategy}\label{s:search-strategy}
The search strategy integrated both controlled \newterm{Medical Subject Headings} (MeSH) and \newterm{free-text terms} pertinent to age-related macular degeneration and anti-angiogenic therapies. \newterm{Boolean operators} (AND, OR) were utilized to refine the search, and truncation or wildcard symbols were employed to capture terminological variations. \subsection{Secondary search}\label{s:secondary-search}
To complement the primary database searches, a secondary search was conducted on \stress{Google Scholar}. This aimed to capture any additional literature that might have been overlooked in the initial search. The secondary search involved keywords and phrases analogous to those used in the primary search, such as `age-related macular degeneration', `anti-VEGF therapies', `angiogenesis inhibitors', and `macular degeneration prevention'. The scope of the secondary search was confined to the first 10 pages of search results, ensuring a manageable yet comprehensive review of pertinent studies.

\subsection{Eligibility criteria}\label{s:eligibility-criteria}
The eligibility criteria for included studies were established based on the PICOS framework:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Population/Patient (P): Individuals diagnosed with AMD.
    \item Intervention (I): Administration of anti-angiogenic therapies aimed at preventing or mitigating AMD progression.
    \item Comparison (C): Various control groups, as determined by the individual study designs.
    \item Outcome (O): Primary outcomes included improvement or stabilization of \newterm{visual acuity}, retinal thickness alterations, and incidence of neovascularization. Secondary outcomes encompassed adverse effects associated with the anti-angiogenic therapies.
    \item Study Design (S): Inclusion was limited to \RCTs, prospective cohort studies, and retrospective cohort studies.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Inclusion criteria}\label{s:inclusion-criteria}
Studies were selected based on the following predefined inclusion criteria:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Original research articles.
    \item Studies involving human subjects with a confirmed diagnosis of AMD.
    \item Investigations assessing the preventive efficacy of anti-angiogenic therapies against AMD.
    \item Publications in peer-reviewed academic journals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Exclusion criteria}\label{s:exclusion-criteria}
The following types of studies were excluded:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Case reports, case series, editorial letters, narrative reviews, meta-analyses, and conference abstracts.
    \item Studies assessing interventions unrelated to anti-angiogenic therapies for AMD.
    \item Unpublished manuscripts or any form of grey literature not subjected to peer review.
    \item Studies devoid of relevant outcome measures pertinent to AMD prevention.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data selection and extraction}\label{s:data-selection-and-extraction}
The selection of articles was meticulously performed in accordance with PRISMA guidelines, encompassing a review of titles, abstracts, and full texts to ascertain their pertinence and quality. Data extraction was systematically carried out using a structured template within a Microsoft Excel spreadsheet. Extracted information included Author(s), Study Design, Study Population, Mean Age of Participants, Type of Intervention, Comparator Group(s), and Primary Results, and Safety Assessment.
